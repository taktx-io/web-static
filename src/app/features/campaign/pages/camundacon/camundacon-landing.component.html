A personal note from the founder, to the people who scanned this at CamundaCon

location_on CamundaCon Amsterdam 2026

You're here because you scanned a QR-code at CamundaCon. That means you're probably already thinking seriously about orchestration, BPMN and workflow at scale. Good — so are we.

Camunda is moving fast in the direction of agentic AI and intelligent automation. That direction is real and the demand is genuine. Orchestration platforms are going to play a significant role in how autonomous processes get deployed and governed in enterprise environments — and we're genuinely excited to see that space mature.

In that world, we are opinionated. When an AI agent makes a decision inside a workflow — can you verify what it decided and why? When a process is modified at runtime — is there a reliable record? When an autonomous action triggers a downstream chain — who authorized it, and can you prove it?

With TaktX we are trying to answer those questions. Opinionated about what the infrastructure underneath AI-assisted orchestration needs to look like. Verifiable execution. Operational visibility that doesn't require an enterprise license. Governance and trust as structural properties, not bolt-ons.

We're in beta and still building. The architectural foundation is open source and available to explore today.

Different by design

Rather than a feature checklist, here's an honest overview of the architectural decisions we made from day one — and why they matter for governance-heavy, high-throughput orchestration workloads.

These are design choices, not feature-complete claims. We're building in public and we're not done.

topic

Kafka-native from day one

Most orchestration engines treat Kafka as an integration option added later. TaktX was built around Kafka from the start — each worker type gets its own dedicated topic, partition counts are independently configurable, and namespace isolation is structural, not a config flag.

draw

Signed commands in the execution model

Signing and verification aren't planned as optional add-ons. They're part of how the engine processes commands — every interaction is attributable and verifiable after the fact.

domain

Infrastructure-level tenant isolation

Multi-tenancy in most platforms is logical separation in a shared runtime. TaktX isolates tenants at the topic namespace level — different runtime topology, not just different records in the same store.

key

Authorization inside the execution flow

JWT authorization isn't applied at the API gateway only — it's woven into the execution model so that trust decisions happen where the work happens, not just at the perimeter.

monitoring

Operational visibility as a first-class concern

Live BPMN heatmaps, sub-second instance state and real-time metrics are not an enterprise add-on. Understanding what's running — and what isn't — is included from the first open source release.

code

Genuinely open source — no artificial limits

Engine and community console are Apache 2.0 with no process limits, no instance caps and no forced upgrade path. The license doesn't change when your load does.

info

We're still building

TaktX is in active beta. The engine, DMN support and community console are available today. The operations console is in development.

Designed for governance-heavy environments

TaktX includes built-in support for signed commands and events, JWT authorization, trust-aware execution and namespace isolation. Designed for organizations where operational trust, auditability and execution integrity matter.

draw

Signed commands & events

Commands and events carry cryptographic signatures. Execution can be verified post-hoc — critical for regulated environments and audit trails.

key

JWT authorization

Every interaction is authorized via JWT tokens. Plug-in your enterprise identity provider (OpenID Connect) with zero code changes.

domain

Namespace isolation

Infrastructure-level multi-tenancy through topic-level namespace isolation. Tenants are separated at the runtime level — not just logically.

history

Auditability by design

Every execution step is recorded and traceable. Built for environments where questions about what happened — and why — must have answers.
