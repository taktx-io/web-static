About TaktX

TaktX started with a simple observation — and a bit of frustration. While working with Camunda 8, it became clear that true horizontal scalability came at a steep price. The architecture was solid, but the licensing and resource requirements made it inaccessible for many teams who simply wanted efficient, scalable process automation. That question — why should performance and affordability be mutually exclusive? — became the spark that started TaktX.

A Lean, High-Performance BPMN Engine

TaktX is a fully distributed BPMN engine built on the Kafka protocol. It runs on Apache Kafka, Confluent Platform, Redpanda, AWS MSK, Axual, and any other Kafka-compatible broker — giving you full freedom to choose your streaming infrastructure. Horizontally scalable, fault-tolerant, and designed from the ground up for throughput, early benchmarks show orders of magnitude higher performance than existing BPMN platforms like Camunda 8 / Zeebe — processing tens of thousands of process instances per second on modest hardware, with sub-100 ms latency.

No Database Dependency
A minimalistic, data-driven architecture that avoids traditional database bottlenecks.
No Central Bottlenecks
Fully distributed flow with components that scale out independently.
Partition-Based Scalability
Horizontal scaling by partition with predictable throughput and latency.

The result? A faster, leaner, and more predictable engine for real-time process orchestration.

Why TaktX

We believe performance and affordability shouldn’t be mutually exclusive. TaktX brings true horizontal scalability to BPMN without the overhead of heavy licenses or complex infrastructure.

Built on modern streaming foundations, it’s engineered for teams who value speed, simplicity, and reliability.
